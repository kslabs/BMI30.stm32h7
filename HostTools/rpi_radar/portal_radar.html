Install beside one antenna, facing the other. Keep the passage empty during the 3-second calibration, then save. Passage range: 60–180 cm. Recalibrate after a large change in antenna spacing. USB and the exposed TX/RX use 115200 baud, 8N1.

If reliable measurements disappear for 0.5 sec, GPIO0 goes high and a sensor fault is shown. Obstacle detection continues independently when the portal is disconnected.
